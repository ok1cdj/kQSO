kQSO is a ham radio logger with a single, smart input line. Type a whole QSO on one line — callsign, band and mode, report, serial, locator, award reference — and kQSO recognizes each part by its shape. A preview under the line shows what will be saved. Logs are plain ADIF files on your device; everything works offline.

Log profiles
• Activation — SOTA, GMA, POTA, WWFF, TOTA lookout towers; your reference is set with the log, the other station's reference is logged for S2S.
• General — adds the name.
• VHF contest — serials, 6-character locator, IARU R1 scoring (points per km), score per band, EDI (REG1TEST) export per band.
• Satellite — one log per pass; the satellite sets uplink / downlink band and frequency.

Logging
• Bands 160 m to 76 GHz, microwaves typed in GHz (24G, 10G …).
• Callsign suggestions and locator prefill from a bundled database and your own worked stations.
• Dupe warning (call + band + mode; contest per band; satellite per bird).
• QSO map of worked locators over the Maidenhead grid, offline.
• Rain radar on the VHF contest map (optional, off by default).
• ADIF export, push to your own Wavelog (API v2).
• Crash-safe: every QSO is written at once, an unfinished one is offered back after a restart.

Radios and keyers (beta)
• Icom IC-705 over Bluetooth: band, mode and exact frequency from the radio, a typed band or F28300 tunes it, CW macros through the radio's keyer, voice memories T1–T4 in SSB / FM, transverters per band.
• CW keyer M5-ESP32-keyer over Bluetooth: macros per profile, RUN / S&P, N1MM-style ESM, CW keyboard.

Own alphabetical keyboard (a hardware keyboard works too), standard and e-ink display modes, light and dark theme, English and Czech.

No ads, no tracking, no account. Free software under GPL-3.0.
