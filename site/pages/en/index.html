What kQSO does

Its own keyboard

Alphabetical, with big keys that work with gloves. The system keyboard and a hardware keyboard work too, with F keys for the macros.

No signal needed

Logging, the map, statistics, the callsign database and nearby references all work offline. The network is only for Wavelog and the chat.

Nothing gets lost

Every QSO is written at once. If the phone crashes or restarts, the QSO you were typing comes back.

Callsign database

After two characters it offers calls and their locator. The VHF part knows the bands a station is usually on, and a dupe shows at once.

Map and statistics

Worked locators on a map, QSOs and points per hour, large squares, the top 10. Optionally a rain radar for rain scatter.

Export and Wavelog

ADIF for SOTA, POTA, WWFF and the others, EDI for VHF contests (a file per band), and a push to your own Wavelog.

E-ink and dark mode

A display mode for e-ink screens (tuned on the Mudita Kompakt), light and dark themes. English and Czech by the system language.

Your data

No account, no server holding your logs, no tracking. Free software under GPL-3.0, the source is on GitHub.
